## Selected Approach: Sunlit Atelier

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial luxury inspired by independent fragrance ateliers, quiet luxury packaging, and East African lifestyle commerce.

### Core Principles
- Warm restraint: cream, blush, and champagne create calm while `#510D41` anchors recognition.
- Editorial asymmetry: offset columns, cropped imagery, and intentional whitespace over generic centered blocks.
- Tactile credibility: soft paper textures, hairline rules, glass reflections, and considered product groupings.
- Human-scale commerce: premium but approachable, with clear prices and direct contact paths.

### Color Philosophy
The deep plum is the brand signature and the visual ink. Warm ivory is the breathing room, blush suggests softness and scent, while champagne gold is used only for small moments of finish. The contrast should feel like sunlight on packaging rather than a neon digital effect.

### Layout Paradigm
A scrolling editorial story: a split hero with product image and message, followed by a horizontal scent personality rail, then an offset product selection grid, and a grounded price/contact panel. Sections should feel composed like magazine spreads rather than uniform cards.

### Signature Elements
- Plum vertical rule and oversized italic serif numerals for section markers.
- Fine champagne-gold hairlines and small uppercase metadata.
- Product imagery framed like a printed atelier sheet with soft shadow and generous margins.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel tactile and confident. Product cards lift subtly on hover, filters become calm pill-like controls, and CTAs respond with a compact press scale. No noisy effects; every motion supports discovery or contact.

### Animation
Use short 180–260ms ease-out transitions for hover and focus. Stagger section reveals by 40ms when motion is allowed, with image opacity and translateY only. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep navigation immediate.

### Typography System
Use Cormorant Garamond for display headlines and editorial labels, plus DM Sans for interface text, prices, and supporting copy. Headlines are high-contrast and compact; body copy is generous with readable line-height; metadata is uppercase with tracked spacing.

### Brand Essence
A premium Kenyan perfume destination for people choosing a scent that feels like them, with designer-inspired oil fragrances, personal guidance, and direct human service. Personality: **cultivated, warm, discerning**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are sensory and self-assured. CTAs are direct, specific, and useful. Microcopy feels personal without being chatty.

Example lines:
- “Find the fragrance that enters the room before you do.”
- “Tell us your mood. We’ll match the moment.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied Trendy Scents Perfumes logo as the exact brand mark. Do not recreate or redraw it; place it as a considered editorial stamp in the header and footer.

### Signature Brand Color
`#510D41` — deep plum, used for navigation emphasis, primary calls to action, dark editorial panels, and packaging-inspired accents.

## Style Decisions
- Use the supplied logo and perfume bottle photograph directly as the hero visual assets.
- Keep the site light-led and tactile rather than dark/neon.
- Avoid fabricated reviews, ratings, or testimonials.
- Keep the storefront frontend-only with enquiry/contact CTAs rather than pretending to process checkout.


## Accepted Review Amendments
- Utility states, including 404 pages, use the Sunlit Atelier system: ivory/blush ground, deep plum CTA, serif-led headline, and warm fragrance-aware microcopy.
- Major sections carry editorial markers such as plum rules, champagne hairlines, oversized serif numerals, or printed-sheet framing.
- The scent personality area uses staggered asymmetry and editorial markers instead of a perfectly uniform card grid.
- Light sections receive subtle material cues through soft image treatments, rules, and shadow framing.
